# Research project page

## Best for

Explaining a project's purpose, activity, people, outputs and opportunities to engage.

## Starter structure

1. Project title and plain-language summary
2. Purpose and research questions
3. Current activity or findings
4. People and partners
5. Publications, data, software or other outputs
6. News and events, where current
7. Funding acknowledgement
8. Contact

Lead with what the work is and why it matters, rather than with organisational history.
