# Contact page

## Best for

Routing visitors to the right person, team or channel with minimal effort.

## Starter structure

1. Page heading and brief scope
2. Contact routes grouped by need
3. Address, opening hours or response expectations where relevant
4. Accessible map or directions only where useful
5. Alternative route for unresolved enquiries

Describe what each contact route is for. Avoid presenting an unexplained directory of names and addresses.
